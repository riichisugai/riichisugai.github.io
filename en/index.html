👋 Hi, I’m Riichi Sugai!
I’m a researcher in computational mechanics, focusing on development of implicit material point method (MPM) addressing plasticity at finite strain and stabilization for nearly incompressible materials. Looooo

🎓 Research Interests#

  • Implicit Material Point Method (MPM) under large deformation
  • Stabilization for nearly incompressible hyperelasticity and plasticity
  • Gradient-enhanced damage model such as the crack phase-field model
  • High performance computing (HPC)

🧩 Current Work#

I’m currently developing a stabilized mixed formulation for implicit MPM for suppressing pressure oscillations.
Next, I’ll try to simulate soil-structure interaction problems.
